About us

Our Team

Meet the people bringing Customs, Shipping, and Logistics experience to the work we do.

Managing Director

Mr. Richard Layne

Mr. Richard Layne founded Layne's Shipping Services Ltd. in April 2011 after retiring from Trinidad and Tobago's Customs and Excise Division. With more than ten years of senior executive experience, he brings extensive knowledge of Customs and Excise matters and has built strong relationships with the agencies responsible for imports and exports.

He is a Licensed Customs Broker who provides consultancy for Warehouses, Special Economic Zones, Transit Sheds, and Excise Bonded Warehouses. He also provides Companies with in-house training, helping staff develop the practical knowledge needed to deliver reliable Customs and Logistics services.

Director

Ms. Angela Sumair-St Louis

Ms. Angela Sumair-St Louis brings 40 years of industry experience to the business, beginning with five years at L.C. Villafana Co. Ltd. and a further five years in documentation preparation, shipping, exports, and import licence requirements.

She later spent five years at Agostini Limited before serving 15 years in a supervisory management role at National Flour Mills Limited in the food manufacturing and pharmaceutical sectors. Her background in operations, documentation, and management has helped shape her contribution to Layne's Shipping Services Limited.

Customs and Excise Consultant

Mr. Imtiaz Ali

Mr. Imtiaz Ali provides specialist expertise in excise and customs matters, with experience across auction sales, warehousing, transit shed operations, and excise warehousing. His work reflects a strong understanding of the practical and regulatory demands of the logistics and customs environment.

After 25 years of service as a Customs and Excise Officer, retiring as a Supervisor, he also served as Secretary of the Customs and the Brokers Board of Trinidad and Tobago. This experience continues to inform his professional guidance and support for clients and colleagues alike.
