About us

Our Team

Meet the people bringing Customs, Shipping, and Logistics experience to the work we do.

Managing Director

Mr. Richard Layne

Mr. Richard Layne founded Layne's Shipping Services Ltd in April 2011 after retiring from the Customs and Excise Division. He brings to the Company more than ten (10) years experience as a Senior Executive in the Industry.

Mr. Layne has extensive knowledge in Customs and Excise matters and has established relationships with Governmental agencies and Private Sector Organizations, having worked at Customs and Excise for 41 years.

Director

Ms. Angela Sumair-St Louis

Ms. Angela Sumair-St Louis brings 40 years of industry experience to the business, beginning her career in the Pharmaceutical Sector with L.C. Villafana and Company Limited where she spent five (5) years as a Grade III (3) Customs Clerk. Thereafter she transitioned to another Pharmaceutical Company, Agostini's Limited where she specialized in Document Preparation, Shipping, Import Licence, and Permit requirements.

In year 2007, she joined the staff at National Flour Mills Limited and continued her career as a Customs Clerk Grade III (3) in the Food Manufacturing Sector where she retired in November 2022. She Brings to our Company her vast Knowledge and experience in the Customs Brokerage field.

Customs and Excise Consultant

Mr. Imtiaz Ali

Mr. Imtiaz Ali is a former Customs and Excise Supervisor who has spent the past twenty-five (25) years in the Customs and Excise Division. Mr. Ali provides specialist expertise in the Customs and Excise matters including the areas of Excise Warehousing, Approved Undertakings, Transit Shed Operations and Auction Sales.

He served as Secretary to the Customs Brokers Board of Trinidad and Tobago prior to his retirement and continues to lend his professional guidance and support to our clients.

Civil Engineer

Rickyle Bynoe

Mr. Rickyle Bynoe, a Civil Engineer and Project Manager with more than six (6) years of experience, is an associate of our company who supports our team projects with the approvals for the establishment of Transit Sheds, Customs Bonded Warehouses and Specialized Economic Zones.

Mr. Bynoe has valuable experience with the provision of Town and Country Planning Division Approvals and other approvals such as Fire Services and OSHA.

Shipping Consultant

Gerard Prudent

Mr Gerard Prudent has over forty (40) years experience in Shipping and Logistics having worked with the Huggins Shipping Group of Companies for thirty-one (31) years. He also worked with Apex Logistics Limited for over nine (9) years. Mr. Prudent at present supports our Company's staff as a Shipping and Logistics Consultant.
