Who we are

Introduction

Laynes Shipping Services Limited. A full service Logistics Provider, located at 1st Floor, Victoria Suites, #14-17 Park Street, Port of Spain, Trinidad, has developed a reputation as a customer- centric, vibrant service organization that is success-driven

Our commitment

Vision statement

Recognizing our responsibility in serving the customers we represent, and the competitiveness in the current market place, Layne's Shipping Services Limited, is committed to providing cost effective solutions to our clients logistics needs.

One of our major objectives, is to operate within the legal framework of Trinidad and Tobago and always ensure our name and the name of our principals always remain associated with Honesty and Integrity.

Our company

Company Overview

Layne's Shipping Services Limited was formed recognizing the need to provide a full service offering to its principals. The aggregate of experience within Layne's, when tallied spans over 60 years in the industry.

We partner with the more reputable service providers on the island and ensure strictest adherence to the prevailing statutory requirements and HSSE Codes

Our History

Company Profile

Layne's Shipping Services Ltd was established in April 2011 after its founder and Managing Director, Mr Richard .R. Layne retired from the Customs and Excise Division, Trinidad and Tobago. With over ten(10) years of experience as a Senior Executive in the industry Mr. Layne has extensive knowledge and experience in Customs and Excise matters and established linkages with the various agencies responsible for imports and exports.

In Addition, Mr. Layne who is a Licensed Broker provides Consultancy Services to companies engaged in the setting up of Warehouses, Special Economics Zones, Transit Sheds and Excise Bonded Warehouses together with in-house training for support staff in these services.
